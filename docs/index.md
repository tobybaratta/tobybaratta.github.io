# Hi, I'm Toby

I’m a third-year PhD student in Informatics at the University of California, Irvine. I study how gaming communities organize, govern themselves, and support one another, with interests in community moderation and how people express compassion and gratitude online. My research is supported by the NSF CSGrad4US fellowship.

Alongside my research, I teach and work in livestream production. Through UCI’s ICS Summer Academy, I taught high school students two short courses in summer 2025: Software Engineering and Software Design. I returned to teach Software Engineering in summer 2026. As a [DTEI Graduate Fellow](https://dtei.uci.edu/opportunities/grads-postdocs/dtei-graduate-fellowship/) in 2025, I helped set up UCI’s Streaming Studio, run the lab, and teach people how to use it for livestreaming.

I’ve presented at the All Things in Moderation conference. In October 2026, I’ll present a poster at Meaningful Play titled **“Understanding gamers’ expressions of compassion and gratitude on Discord.”**

I live in Irvine, California, with my two cats, **Alfie** and **Lucy**. Outside of work, I enjoy video games, side projects, and reading.

[See what I’m working on now](./now.md), [view my CV](./cv.md), or [get in touch](./contact.md).
